How to use this book
  • Personalize the first line, every time. The italic fill-ins are where you name something specific about the person. If you can't fill them, you're not ready to contact that person yet.
  • One channel per prospect to start. Call businesses (they answer phones), email practitioners, DM creators. The contact CSVs tell you which.
  • Give before you ask. Every script offers free access first — value, not a pitch.
  • Cap & log. 10–20 personalized sends a day, max. Log every touch in partner-outreach-tracker.csv.
  • Three touches, then stop. Never more. A fast "no" is a win.

📞 Phone & Voicemail

Section 1 · Live calls & voicemails

Businesses answer phones — therapists, clinics, retreat centers, small HR teams. The goal of a first call is almost never a sale; it's an email address to send a free demo to, a 15-minute follow-up, or a clean "no." Keep every call to 60–120 seconds.

Before you dial
  • Smile — it's audible. Stand up. Have their name and one specific detail in front of you.
  • Call in blocks (10 in a row); you warm up after the first two.
  • If they're busy: "No problem — what's the best email? I'll send it over." Still a win.

The 20-second cold-call opener

Goal: a name + email to send the demo to.
"Hi, this is Gabriel — I'll be quick. I'm the founder of a self-reflection app called Mirror, and I work with {therapists / coaches / counselors} who want something structured for the people they serve between sessions. I'm not selling anything — I'd just love to give your team free access to look at. Who's the best person to send that to?"
Then
  • Get a name + email → send the therapist email that same day → log it.

Cold call — Therapist / Coach

Goal: capture an email. Time: 60–90 seconds.
"Hi, is this [name]? — Hey [name], my name is Gabriel, I'm the founder of Mirror. It's a personal intelligence app my clients — sorry, my users — use between sessions with people like you. I'm calling therapists in [city] this week for feedback, not to sell you anything. Do you have 90 seconds? [wait for yes] Mirror is a phone app your clients can use to journal daily with real emotion tracking, take a full personality and reflection intake, and get anonymous feedback from other users about how they come across. The angle is: it gives your clients structure between the times they see you, and it gives you a shared reference point when they come in. Two questions. One — does that sound like something you'd want to look at? And two — if I gave you a lifetime free Premium account to evaluate, what's the best email to send that to? [capture email, confirm spelling, tell them you'll follow up next week to hear what they thought.]"

Cold call — Retreat organizer

Goal: send a free founder's account. Time: 90–120 seconds.
"Hi, is this [name]? Hi [name], I'm Gabriel, founder of Mirror. I saw you run [retreat name] — the one in [month/place]. Quick question, not a sales call: do your attendees currently do any intake or integration between the time they book and the time they show up? [listen] Reason I ask — I built a personal reflection app that a few retreat leaders have started using as a pre-retreat prep and post-retreat integration tool. It captures a personality and emotional baseline before, and gives them a way to journal and get grounded after. I'd love to send you a free founder's account so you can look at it. Would that be useful?"

Cold call — HR / People-ops (small company)

Goal: send a one-pager / pilot. Keep it crisp.
"Hi, is this [name]? Hey [name], I'm Gabriel, founder of a small wellness product called Mirror. I'm calling because we're pricing a team plan and I only need to talk to 8 people. Is that you? [if not, ask who and get referred out] Very quick — Mirror is $10 per employee per month. What people get is a personal reflection dashboard, private journaling, and the option to form small anonymous feedback groups inside the company. It's not a mental health tool, it's a self-awareness one, so there's no PHI liability for you. I'll send you a one-pager if it's interesting. Best email?"

Podcast pitch — voicemail-safe

Goal: plant the hook; the email does the closing.
"Hi [name], this is Gabriel — I'm the founder of Mirror, the app that gives you a perception-gap score based on anonymous peer reviews of your personality. I've been a listener of [show name] since [episode] and I think your audience would like the story of why we built our own AI to do this. I'll follow up by email, but if you want to skip the queue, my number is [your callback number]. Thanks for the show."

The warm-intro call — after an email reply

Goal: a referral link + one introduction.
"Hey [name], thanks for getting back to me. You mentioned you looked at the intake — what did you think? [shut up. let them talk. take notes.] Great, that's really useful. Two things I want to leave you with. One, if this fits your practice, I'll set you up with a referral link so you can pass Premium codes to five clients to start. Two, is there anyone else in your network — another therapist, a coach, a group leader — who'd want to hear about this? A one-sentence intro from you is worth 50 of my cold calls."

The break-up call — after 3 tries, no reply

Goal: a "no" with a reason, or a surprise "yes." Works as an email too.
"Hey [name] — closing the loop on this, I don't want to be a pest. If it's not a fit right now, totally fair, and I'd love to know what would have made it a fit. Two lines back is enough. Either way, I'll leave you alone after this. Thanks for the time."
Voicemail note
  • Line 5's [your callback number] is the one blank you must fill — use a free Google Voice number, not a personal cell, so callbacks stay separate.

✉️ Email

Section 2 · Sent 1:1 from your inbox

Send these by hand from gabrielgomez@trymirror.world, one at a time — never mail-merge. Personalize the first two sentences, keep it under ~130 words, one easy ask, free access offered. This is not the bulk campaign engine.

Universal rules — apply to every email
  • Personalize the first two sentences. Reference their actual work. If you can't, you're not ready to send.
  • Lead with them, not you. Mirror shows up as useful to their audience, not a pitch.
  • One low-effort ask · under ~130 words · free access offered · signed as you, with the site link.
  • Add the opt-out line at the bottom: "Reply 'no thanks' and I won't write again." Honor it instantly.
  • Cap 10–20/day. Above that, quality drops and domain reputation is at risk.

The universal short email

Goal: a reply and a free-access yes. Works for any practitioner.
Subject: A quiet tool for the people you work with
Hi {first name}, I came across your work in {their focus — e.g. "measurement-based therapy" / "training new coaches" / "guiding retreat guests"}, and it's the reason I'm writing. I'm the founder of Mirror — a personal-reflection tool that helps someone see themselves clearly: their personality and patterns, how they think and feel, and even how others actually perceive them. Practitioners are pointing people to it for structure between sessions. It's a mirror, never a replacement for the work you do. I'm not selling anything today — I'd value your eye on it. Could I set you up with free access to try? If it helps the people you serve, wonderful; if not, your honest read would help me more than you know. Warmly, Gabriel Gomez · Founder, Mirror · trymirror.world Reply 'no thanks' and I won't write again.

📱 Social & DM

Section 3 · Direct messages & post CTAs

For creators (MBTI / astrology / self-dev on TikTok, IG, YouTube). DMs are personal, never copy-paste — platforms flag identical text and creators can smell it. Change the first line every single time.

DM etiquette
  • One message, no wall of text.
  • No link in the first DM if the platform buries it — offer to send it when they reply.
  • Never mass-send identical text. Reference a specific recent post so it's obviously human.
  • Reply fast when they respond — momentum is everything in DMs.

The 3-line creator DM

Goal: a free founder link → possible collab.
Line 1 (personal hook): "Your post on {specific thing they said} genuinely stopped my scroll — {one honest sentence about why}." Line 2 (the bridge): "I built something in that exact spirit: Mirror, a tool that reflects back who someone actually is — personality, how they think and feel, even the gap between how they see themselves and how others see them." Line 3 (the ask, tiny): "No pitch — I'd love to just give you a free founder link to poke at. If it resonates, maybe it's something your audience would love too. Either way, thank you for the work you put out."

Short creator DM (one-liner)

Goal: a fast yes when you only get one line of attention.
Hey {handle} — your {specific post/series} is great, the {specific detail} bit especially. I built Mirror, an app that profiles personality + cognition + astrology and shows how others actually perceive you (anonymous peer review with a perception-gap score). Feels made for your audience. Can I give you free access to try — and if you like it, talk about a collab? No pressure either way.
If they reply positively
  • Move to email for specifics — free codes, timing, and what a partnership looks like (affiliate %, a sponsored post, or a giveaway).
