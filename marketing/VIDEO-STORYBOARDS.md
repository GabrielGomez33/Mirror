# Mirror — Video Storyboards (Week 1)

Shot-by-shot, production-ready storyboards for the first five videos, built for
**ElevenLabs** (voice + music) and **AI video generation** (ElevenLabs video or
any text-to-video tool). Each video is 15–30s, vertical (1080×1920), designed to
be produced in ~30–45 min once the brand kit below is set.

> Source concepts + strategy live in `CONTENT-PLAYBOOK.md`. This doc is the
> "sit down and make it" layer. Produce all five in one batch session, then
> schedule one per weekday.

---

## Brand kit (set once, reuse in every video)

**Character — "Little One":** a small, round, soft cartoon figure with big
expressive eyes and simple stub limbs. Think a gentle pastel blob-person. Keep
it *simple* — simple characters stay consistent across AI-generated shots.

**Palette:** sakura pink `#fff0f5`, rose `#e8557c`, deep burgundy `#3b1220`,
warm cream. Soft, calm, lots of negative space.

**Type (captions/on-screen text):** a clean rounded sans (e.g. Poppins/Nunito),
bold, centered, one line at a time, burgundy on light or white on rose.

**Motion:** slow, gentle, floaty. No fast cuts. Calm is the brand.

**ElevenLabs voice (reuse the SAME voice every time):**
- Pick one warm, calm, unhurried narration voice from the library (search
  "calm," "warm," "soft," "narration").
- Suggested settings (tune to taste): **Stability 50, Similarity 80, Style
  15, Speaker Boost ON.** Low style = calm, not performative.
- Pace it slow. Insert pauses with SSML-style breaks or ellipses in the script,
  e.g. `You've looked in a mirror a thousand times. <break time="0.6s"/> But
  you've never seen this.`

**ElevenLabs music:** one soft, warm, ambient bed per video (search "calm,"
"gentle," "reflective," "lo-fi warm"). Keep it LOW (about -18 dB under the VO).
Half a beat of silence right before the CTA line.

**AI video-gen style prefix (paste before every scene prompt):**
> *Soft 2D cartoon animation, minimal and hand-drawn, sakura-pink and rose
> palette on a warm cream background, gentle floaty motion, cozy and calm mood,
> a small round pastel character with big expressive eyes, lots of negative
> space, no on-screen text.*

**AI-video reality check (important):** text-to-video struggles to keep a
character identical across separate clips. Mitigate: generate **short 2–4s
clips per scene**, keep the character description **identical** each time, reuse
a seed/reference image if the tool supports it, and lean on the mirror + palette
for continuity. If a shot won't behave, fall back to animating that beat in
Canva/CapCut with a static Little One — consistency beats realism here.

**Every video ends the same way (brand end-card, ~2s):** the Mirror wordmark +
`trymirror.world` on a soft rose field. Reuse the exact same end-card clip.

## Batch production checklist (one session, ~2.5–3 hrs)

1. [ ] Lock the ElevenLabs **voice** + settings; save it as your project voice.
2. [ ] Generate all 5 **voiceovers** (paste scripts with the `<break>` pauses).
3. [ ] Pick 1 **music bed** per video; set to ~-18 dB under the VO.
4. [ ] Generate **video clips** scene-by-scene (style prefix + each shot prompt).
       Keep clips 2–4s; regenerate any that break character.
5. [ ] Assemble in your editor: clips → VO → music → **burned-in captions** →
       the shared **end-card**.
6. [ ] Export 1080×1920, one file per video.
7. [ ] Schedule one/weekday across TikTok + Reels + Shorts with the caption pack.
8. [ ] Post #1 (perception gap) or #2 (3am) first — highest recognition.

## After week 1
Whichever video earns the most **saves + shares**, make three more in that vein
next week (the `CONTENT-PLAYBOOK.md` has 10 concepts to pull from). Let the
audience vote with saves — that's your "true mirror" message finding its people.
