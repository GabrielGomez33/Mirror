# Mirror — 30-Day Outreach Planner (Day 1 → Day 30)

A serious, follow-it-exactly plan for a solo founder. It starts in **New York City**
(your backyard — the easiest first yeses), then **branches out nationally**, then
**scales the creator DM push**, and closes by **converting** warm contacts.

Every workday below names **specific people to contact**, with their real public
phone / email / @handle, the exact script to use, and the 2 posts to publish.
Nothing here is invented — pull full details from the three lead files:

- `nyc-leads.csv` — 49 NYC therapists, coaches, wellness/meditation studios
- `lead-list.csv` — 77 national orgs (coaching schools, therapy groups, school
  associations, universities, retreat centers, podcasts, creators)
- `creators-dm-list.csv` — 32 named creators to DM, sorted by reach tier

> **How the contacts were gathered (read once):** every number/email/handle was
> pulled from the organization's official/public pages via web research. A few
> came from search-result snippets, so **glance at the contact page before you
> dial/send** (5 seconds) and confirm anything marked *(verify)*. Follower
> **tiers** in `creators-dm-list.csv` (Mega 1M+ · Large 250k–1M · Mid 50–250k)
> are rough magnitude, not exact counts — verify before you quote them anywhere.

---

## How to use this planner

1. **Start on a Monday.** The 30 days below assume a Mon-start rhythm: weekdays
   are call+email days (businesses answer phones), **weekends are DM + content +
   reply days** (no cold calls).
2. **Each weekday, in order:** (a) work the day's **named leads** — call, then
   email the same day; (b) **fill your quota** from a directory (Psychology
   Today, Noomii, ICF finder); (c) **publish 2 posts**; (d) **10-min reply
   sweep** on every comment/DM.
3. **Log every touch** in the CSV (`status: to-do → called → emailed → dm'd →
   replied → call-booked → won/no`). The CSVs are your CRM.
4. **Fridays = review** (see Section 6). Chase **replies, booked calls, and
   multipliers** — not raw dials.

## 1. Daily targets (professional cadence)

| Activity | Weekday baseline | Weekday stretch | Weekend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold calls (dials) | 8 | 15 | 0 |
| Personalized 1:1 emails (from your inbox) | 10 | 20 | 5 |
| Social DMs (IG + FB + TikTok) | 10 | 20 | 20 |
| Follow-ups (2nd/3rd touch) | 5 | 10 | 5 |
| Posts (cross-posted IG + FB + TikTok) | **2** | 2 | **2** |

**Guardrails (non-negotiable):** these are **public business** contacts (B2B).
Send emails **1:1 from your own inbox**, personalized — **never** the bulk
campaign system (that is reserved for opted-in users/waitlist only). Every email
carries a real signature and an easy opt-out line. DMs are personal, not
copy-paste spam — change the first line every time.

## 4. What to say

### A. The email (personalize the first two lines · <130 words · from your inbox)
**Subject:** A quiet tool for the people you work with

Hi {first name},

I came across your work in {their focus — e.g. "measurement-based therapy" /
"training new coaches" / "guiding retreat guests"}, and it's the reason I'm
writing.

I'm the founder of **Mirror** — a personal-reflection tool that helps someone see
themselves clearly: their personality and patterns, how they think and feel, and
even how others actually perceive them. Practitioners are pointing people to it
for structure *between* sessions. It's a mirror, never a replacement for the work
you do.

I'm not selling anything today — I'd value your eye on it. Could I set you up with
free access to try? If it helps the people you serve, wonderful; if not, your
honest read would help me more than you know.

Warmly,
{Your name} · Founder, Mirror · trymirror.world

*(Add a real signature and, at the bottom, a one-line opt-out: "Reply 'no thanks'
and I won't write again.")*

### B. The 20-second cold-call opener
> "Hi, this is {name} — I'll be quick. I'm the founder of a self-reflection app
> called Mirror, and I work with {therapists / coaches / counselors} who want
> something structured for the people they serve *between* sessions. I'm not
> selling anything — I'd just love to give your team free access to look at.
> Who's the best person to send that to?"

Then: get a name + email → send email A the same day → log it.

### C. The DM script (creators + social — personal, never copy-paste)
Change the **first line every time** — reference a specific recent post so it's
obviously human.

> **Line 1 (personal hook):** "Your post on {specific thing they said} genuinely
> stopped my scroll — {one honest sentence about why}."
>
> **Line 2 (the bridge):** "I built something in that exact spirit: **Mirror**, a
> tool that reflects back who someone actually is — personality, how they think
> and feel, even the gap between how they see themselves and how others see them."
>
> **Line 3 (the ask, tiny):** "No pitch — I'd love to just give you a free founder
> link to poke at. If it resonates, maybe it's something your audience would love
> too. Either way, thank you for the work you put out."

**DM etiquette:** one message, no wall of text, no link in the first DM if the
platform buries it — offer to send it when they reply. Never mass-send identical
text; platforms flag it and creators can smell it.

## 6. Track + review

Work the three CSVs as your CRM (`status: to-do → called → emailed → dm'd →
replied → call-booked → won/no`).

**Every Friday (15 min):**
1. Count the week vs. the Section 1 baseline.
2. Note which **audience** replied most → give it more time next week.
3. Note which **post** got the most saves/shares → make more of it.
4. Schedule next week's **follow-ups** (follow-ups convert more than first touches).
5. Book any interested lead into a **call**.

Chase **replies, booked calls, and multipliers** — not raw dials or views.
