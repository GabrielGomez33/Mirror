# Mirror — Prospect List (Week 1–4 Working Set)

> Every target below is real and publicly listed. Contact info is what
> the target publishes on their own site or platform bio — you still
> need to click through and grab the current email / DM form yourself.
>
> A few ground rules:
>
> - Do **not** cold-call individual therapists' personal cell numbers.
>   Call the practice front desk from Psychology Today, or email the
>   practice email.
> - Do **not** paste the same DM to 20 creators. Pull one specific
>   detail from their latest post and reference it.
> - When a target has an obvious "for business inquiries" email in
>   their bio, use it. That's their invitation.
> - This list is a **starter**. Your job in Weeks 2–4 is to expand
>   each row into 20–50 similar targets.

---

## Section A — Directories to mine (build your lead list here)

Every hour spent scraping these is more valuable than an hour on any
single call in Week 1. Pull to a spreadsheet and export to your
outreach tool.

### Therapists / mental health professionals
- **Psychology Today – Find a Therapist** — the largest directory.
  Filter by city, then by "In-Network / Private Pay" (private pay
  practices are your target; they don't need insurance approval to
  try new tools). Also filter by specialty ("Anxiety",
  "Relationship Issues", "Life Transitions") — those specialties
  match Mirror's use case.
  https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/therapists
- **TherapyDen** — smaller, more progressive practitioner base.
  Filter by specialty + "LGBTQ+ friendly" or "Trauma-informed" —
  those cohorts over-index on trying new tools.
  https://www.therapyden.com/
- **Zencare** — curated East Coast + California directory. Higher
  end / private-pay skewing. Practices listed here are usually
  established and open to referrals.
  https://zencare.co/
- **GoodTherapy** — broader net, includes coaches. Use for
  supplementary volume.
  https://www.goodtherapy.org/find-therapist.html
- **Open Path Collective** — sliding-scale practitioners. Younger
  clientele, higher openness to new tools.
  https://openpathcollective.org/

**Playbook:** Start with your own city + one adjacent city. Pull 50
therapists into a sheet with: name, practice email, specialty, phone,
website. Batch outreach 10/day.

### Coaches
- **ICF (International Coaching Federation) Credentialed Coach
  Finder** — the gold-standard directory of credentialed life &
  executive coaches. Filter by niche.
  https://experience.coachingfederation.org/find-a-coach
- **BetterUp Coach Directory** — coaches vetted through a network,
  higher trust.
  https://www.betterup.com/
- **Noomii** — general life-coach directory.
  https://www.noomii.com/

### Retreat centers & wellness venues
- **Retreat Guru** — 500+ retreat centers in the US with contact
  info; filter by type (silent, women's, couples', yoga, plant
  medicine, etc.).
  https://retreat.guru/be/meditation-retreats/united-states
- **BookRetreats.com** — retreat directory; every listing has a
  contact email and organizer name.
  https://bookretreats.com/s/wellness-retreats/united-states
- **Tripaneer** — additional retreat marketplace.
  https://www.tripaneer.com/all/c/meditation/d/usa
- **Dharmakaya Center for Wellbeing** (Cragsmoor, NY) — named as
  a top US wellness retreat by USA Today. Silent, spiritual, small
  cohorts. Positioning: pre + post integration.
- **Ratna Ling** (Sonoma County, CA) — Buddhist non-profit retreat
  center. Same play.
- **Osage Forest of Peace** (Tulsa, OK) — small, interfaith,
  approachable.
- **Sedona AZ retreat cluster** — dozens of independent retreat
  operators; search Retreat Guru "Sedona." Aesthetic + audience fit
  is nearly perfect.

**Playbook:** Book Retreats and Retreat Guru will give you 100+
organizer email addresses in an afternoon. Prioritize centers hosting
retreats in the next 60–90 days (they need engagement tools *now*).

### Podcasts (see Section B below for named targets)
- **Podchaser** — every podcast has a contact page. Filter by
  category.
  https://www.podchaser.com/categories/astrology-category/podcasts
- **Podcast Guest Collaboration Facebook Groups** — free, high
  quality. Search "Podcast Guests Wanted" or "Podcast Interview
  Guests."
- **Podmatch / MatchMaker.fm** — paid guest-matching tools, worth
  the $25/mo if you're pitching 20+ shows.

### Small-group leaders (masterminds, book clubs, faith groups)
- **Meetup.com** — search "book club", "personal development",
  "men's group", "women's circle" in your city. Every organizer has
  a message form.
- **Facebook Groups** — search "mastermind" + your niche. Comment
  first, DM the admin second.

### Small-company HR & people ops
- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** trial (free 30 days) — filter
  companies 20–200 employees, then title:
  `"People Operations" OR "Head of People" OR "HR Manager"`.
  This is the fastest B2B pipeline for the Enterprise / team play.

## Section B — Named podcasts to pitch (guest spots)

Every one of these accepts guest pitches. Pitch angle noted. Contact
via the show's website — usually a "Pitch a Guest" or "Contact" page.
When you email, reference a specific recent episode in the first
sentence.

### Astrology (your closest aesthetic + audience match)

1. **The Astrology Podcast** — host Chris Brennan. Deep, academic,
   loves technique + history. **Pitch angle:** "we built a product
   that treats natal placements as first-class data alongside Big
   Five personality." He'll be intrigued by the integration.
   https://theastrologypodcast.com/
2. **Ghost of a Podcast: Astrology & Advice** — host Jessica
   Lanyadoo. Warmer, advice-column style. **Pitch angle:** "founder
   who lets anonymous strangers rate her personality — what would
   you say to me?" She'll bite on the narrative.
3. **Astrology Hub** — Amanda Pua Walsh. Business-savvy audience
   of practicing astrologers. **Pitch angle:** "we integrate
   Western, Chinese, and West African astrology — the market is
   ready for pluralism."
   https://astrologyhub.com/podcast/
4. **Cosmic Cousins** — Jeff Hinshaw. Queer / trans-inclusive
   audience. Aesthetic aligns.
5. **Stars Like Us** — Astrologer Aliza Kelly. Fashion + culture
   crossover audience.
6. **Astrology Bytes** — short-format, easy first "yes." Good for
   getting on the reel.
7. **Allegedly Astrology** — pop culture / true crime crossover.
   Not a fit for deep pitch, but they'll do fun segments.

### Personality typology (MBTI, Enneagram)

8. **Typology** — Ian Morgan Cron. Enneagram-focused,
   psychotherapist host. Audience is high-openness, high-earning.
   **Pitch angle:** "we quantify the perception gap — the
   Enneagram's blind-spot problem, solved."
   https://www.typologypodcast.com/
9. **The Enneagram Journey** — Suzanne Stabile. Institutional
   Enneagram audience.
10. **Enneagram 2.0** — Beatrice Chestnut & Uranio Paes. Global,
    academic.
11. **Fit Enneagram with Hallie Ringhand** — health & fitness
    crossover.

### Self-development (broader; harder to get on but bigger)

12. **On Purpose with Jay Shetty** — massive; try only after you
    have a launched marketing site + press hits. Not a Week-1 pitch.
13. **The Tim Ferriss Show** — same caveat.
14. **The School of Greatness** — Lewis Howes. Same caveat.
15. **The Rich Roll Podcast** — mid-reach fit for a founder-story
    angle.
16. **Optimal Living Daily** — read your article on their show.
    Easiest first "yes" — you just need to write a good short essay
    and submit.
    https://oldpodcast.com/
17. **Hidden Brain** — Shankar Vedantam. Would love the
    perception-gap data angle. Long lead time.
18. **Huberman Lab** — long shot but perfect for the on-device ML
    + emotion detection story. Send a *short* email with the
    technical hook.

### Relationships & couples

19. **Where Should We Begin? with Esther Perel** — dream target,
    long lead. Save for month 6.
20. **Reimagining Love with Dr. Alexandra Solomon** — very
    approachable, actively takes guests.
21. **Relationship Advice** with Colter, Cayla, and Lauren — three
    LPCs; probably the best call for MirrorGroups-for-couples pitch.
22. **Dear Young Married Couple** — Adam & Karissa King. Newlywed
    audience, sticky. Good for the MirrorGroups pitch.
23. **Foreplay Radio – Couples and Sex Therapy** — big evergreen
    audience.
24. **The Therapy Crouch** — Abbey Clancy & Peter Crouch. UK, mass
    audience.

**Playbook:** Pitch 5 shows per week. Personalize each pitch to a
*specific recent episode.* Reply rate on cold podcast pitches is
~5–15% if the pitch is specific, ~1% if it's generic.

and only mention the tool in a top comment when someone asks.

## Section E — Corporate wellness & benefits (B2B channel)

Small companies with 20–200 employees are your entry — the mid-market
where a $10/mo per-employee decision doesn't need a procurement
process.

### Target roles on LinkedIn
- "Head of People" / "People Operations" / "HR Manager"
- "Head of Employee Experience"
- "Wellness Program Manager"
- "Culture Manager"

### Industries with highest expected fit
- **Design / creative agencies** — Big Five + reflection lands
  culturally.
- **Software / SaaS 20–100 people** — introspective founders,
  budget for wellness, low procurement friction.
- **Non-profits** (esp. mental-health-adjacent) — mission alignment.
- **Yoga / studio chains** — could resell to their members.
- **Boutique consulting** — team dynamics are the product.

### Corporate wellness marketplaces to get listed on

- **Awardco** — https://www.award.co/

  wellness benefit.
