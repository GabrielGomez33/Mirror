# Mirror — Partner Outreach Kit

**Purpose:** the highest-EV marketing channel at your stage is *personalized 1:1
outreach to multipliers* — people who each put Mirror in front of many potential
users. This kit gives you ready-to-send templates, a sending discipline, and a
tracker. Send these from **your own inbox and LinkedIn**, by hand, personalized.
This is **not** cold-email-at-scale (that's a later, separate motion — see the
Solo-Founder Playbook). One thoughtful message beats fifty automated ones.

> Pair this with `PROSPECT-LIST.md` (who to contact) and
> `SOLO-FOUNDER-OUTREACH-PLAYBOOK.md` (daily rhythm + budget).

## Universal rules (read once, apply to every send)

1. **Personalize the first two sentences, always.** Reference their actual work
   — the episode you heard, the post they wrote, their practice's focus. If you
   can't name something specific, you're not ready to send to that person yet.
2. **Lead with them, not you.** The first line is about their world; Mirror
   shows up as something useful *to their audience*, not a pitch for your app.
3. **One ask, easy to say yes to.** A reply, a quick call, or a free login — not
   "please promote my product."
4. **Short.** 90–130 words. If they have to scroll, you lost.
5. **From your real identity.** Your name, founder, real signature, link to
   `theundergroundrailroad.world`. No-reply addresses and mail-merge artifacts
   kill trust.
6. **Give before you ask.** Offer them free Premium/TruthStream access to try —
   value first.
7. **Volume cap:** 10–20 *personalized* sends/day from your own inbox, max. Above
   that, quality drops and your domain reputation is at risk — that's the
   threshold where you'd move cold volume to dedicated infrastructure, not
   before.

**Personalization checklist before hitting send:**
- [ ] Named something specific about them in the first 2 sentences
- [ ] The ask is a single, low-effort yes
- [ ] Under ~130 words
- [ ] A real reason *their audience* benefits (not just "my app is cool")
- [ ] Free-access offer included
- [ ] Signed as you, with the site link

## Template 1 — Podcast host

**Subject options**
- `Loved your episode on {topic} — an idea for your audience`
- `{Show name} + a self-knowledge tool built on real data`

**Body**
> Hi {First name},
>
> Your recent episode on {specific topic / guest} stuck with me — {one genuine,
> specific sentence proving you actually listened}.
>
> I built Mirror, a personal-intelligence app that maps who someone is across
> personality, cognition, emotion, and cosmology, then lets them see how others
> actually perceive them (anonymous peer review with a "perception-gap" score).
> It's exactly the kind of self-knowledge your listeners lean into.
>
> Would you be open to me setting you up with free access to try it? If it
> resonates, I'd love to explore coming on the show — no pressure either way.
>
> Either way, thanks for the work you put out.
>
> — {Your name}, founder · theundergroundrailroad.world

**Follow-up (if no reply, +5 days):**
> Hi {First name} — floating this back up in case it slipped by. Happy to just
> send you a free login so you can poke around, no strings. Loved {other
> specific thing}. — {Your name}

## Template 2 — Therapist / coach

**Subject options**
- `A between-session tool for your clients (free to try)`
- `Built a self-reflection app — would value your read`

**Body**
> Hi {First name},
>
> I came across your practice — {specific: their niche, e.g. "your focus on
> attachment work with couples"} — and thought you might have a sharp opinion on
> something I built.
>
> Mirror is a self-reflection app: structured journaling with mood/emotion
> tracking, a personality-and-cognition profile, and AI-guided reflection. Some
> clinicians are using it with clients as light structure *between* sessions —
> never a replacement for care, just a mirror.
>
> Could I give you free Premium access to evaluate it? If it's useful to your
> clients, wonderful; if not, your honest feedback would genuinely help me.
>
> — {Your name}, founder · theundergroundrailroad.world

**Note:** never imply clinical/diagnostic claims. Position as *reflection &
structure*, not treatment.

## Follow-up cadence (all types except creators)

| Touch | When | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 | The template above |
| 2 | Day +5 | Short bump — re-offer the *free access*, add one new specific detail |
| 3 | Day +12 | "Break-up" — "I'll stop here; if it's ever useful, the door's open: {link}" (this one gets surprisingly high reply rates) |

Three touches, then stop. Never more. Log every touch in the tracker.

---

## The tracker

Use `partner-outreach-tracker.csv` (in this folder) — import it into Google
Sheets. One row per prospect. Columns:

- **name / org** — who
- **type** — podcast / therapist / coach / facilitator / creator / corporate
- **channel** — email / LinkedIn / IG DM / etc.
- **contact** — email or handle
- **personalization_hook** — the specific thing you'll reference (fill BEFORE sending)
- **status** — `to-send` → `sent` → `replied` → `call-booked` → `won` / `no`
- **touch1_date / touch2_date / touch3_date**
- **free_access_sent** — codes/logins you gave them
- **next_action / next_date**
- **notes**

**Weekly targets (solo, ~3.5 hrs/day):** 50 personalized touches out, 3–5
conversations booked. Review the tracker every Friday: who to follow up, what
hook worked, which type is replying most — then do more of that.

## What "good" looks like after 30 days

- 150–200 personalized touches sent (across the five types)
- 15–40 replies (10–20% is healthy for well-personalized outreach)
- 5–10 real conversations / calls
- 1–3 committed multipliers (a podcast booking, a therapist recommending you, a
  creator collab, or a corporate pilot)

One committed multiplier can outperform a month of paid ads. That's why this
channel is worth doing by hand, now.
