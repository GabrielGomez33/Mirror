# Mirror — Content Creation Playbook

A repeatable system for making short videos that attract people seeking growth
and self-knowledge — "those looking for a true mirror." Built for one person
producing daily with ElevenLabs for voice/music and a simple animation tool for
the visuals.

> Pairs with `PARTNER-OUTREACH-KIT.md` (1:1 outreach) and
> `SOLO-FOUNDER-OUTREACH-PLAYBOOK.md` (budget + rhythm). This doc is the
> **content engine**; those are the **relationship engine**.

---

## The creative north star

**Mirror is the app that shows you who you actually are — and how the world
actually sees you.** Every piece of content should make a stranger feel one of
two things:

1. *"That's me."* (recognition — a named feeling they've never seen named)
2. *"I want to know that about myself."* (curiosity — the pull of self-knowledge)

If a video doesn't create recognition or curiosity in the first 2 seconds, it
won't travel. Lead with the feeling, reveal Mirror at the end.

**Tone:** warm, a little poetic, never clinical, never hustle-y. Cute but not
childish. Think "a gentle friend who sees you clearly."

---

## The signature visual: the Mirror world

A tiny recurring cast of **simple round cartoon figures** (think soft blobs with
expressive eyes — cheap to animate, instantly readable, screenshot-friendly).
The one prop that ties every video together: **a mirror.** What the figure sees
in the mirror is the story.

- **Little One** — the everyperson figure. The viewer.
- **The Reflection** — what the mirror shows: sometimes truer, deeper, glowing.
- **The Others** — small background figures whose *perceptions* matter (for the
  TruthStream / perception-gap stories).

Palette: your sakura/zen brand — soft pinks, rose, warm cream, one deep accent.
Keep it calm and uncluttered. Lots of negative space. One idea per video.

---

## Content pillars (rotate these)

1. **Recognition** — name a feeling/pattern people haven't seen named. ("The
   3am overthinker," "the person who's the therapist of the friend group.")
   → highest shareability. Do the most of these.
2. **The perception gap** — how you see you vs. how others see you. This is
   TruthStream, your most unique feature. Deeply shareable and a little
   provocative. → your signature pillar.
3. **The five layers** — personality, mind, emotion, voice/face, cosmos. Curiosity
   fuel: "there are five versions of you and you've only met one."
4. **Growth / becoming** — the quiet glow-up of self-awareness. Aspirational.
5. **Cosmos meets science** — astrology + psychology together. Rides existing
   astrology/MBTI audiences on TikTok/IG.

Weekly mix: 3 Recognition · 1 Perception gap · 1 of the rest. Perception-gap
posts are your "swing for the fences" — post one every week without fail.

## 10 ready-to-produce scripts

Format for each: **Hook** (first line, on-screen + spoken) · **Visual** ·
**Voiceover** (ElevenLabs) · **On-screen text** · **CTA**. Target 15–30s.

### 1. The Mirror That Talks Back  *(Pillar: Recognition)*
- **Hook:** "What if your mirror could tell you the truth?"
- **Visual:** Little One nervously approaches a mirror. The reflection blooms
  into soft glowing words — *curious, guarded, deeply loyal, afraid of being
  boring.* Little One's eyes widen.
- **VO:** "You've looked in a mirror a thousand times. But you've never seen
  *this.* Your personality. Your mind. The way you love. Written back to you —
  gently, honestly. This is Mirror."
- **Text:** "meet the real you"
- **CTA:** "Step inside → trymirror.world"

### 2. How You See You vs. How They See You  *(Pillar: Perception gap — signature)*
- **Hook:** "You think you come across as calm. They think you're intimidating."
- **Visual:** Split screen. Left: Little One sees itself small and shy. Right:
  The Others see it tall and glowing. A number appears between them: a
  "perception gap."
- **VO:** "There's a gap between how you see yourself and how the world sees
  you. Most people never get to measure it. Mirror lets you — anonymously,
  honestly. It might be the most useful thing you learn this year."
- **Text:** "what's your gap?"
- **CTA:** "Find out → trymirror.world"

### 3. The 3am Overthinker  *(Pillar: Recognition)*
- **Hook:** "It's 3am and your brain is holding a meeting no one asked for."
- **Visual:** Little One lies awake, tiny thought-figures swirling above it
  (a text you sent, a thing from 2019, a 'what did they mean by that'). Then the
  mirror glows softly beside the bed and the swirl slows.
- **VO:** "If your mind never clocks out — you're not broken. You're just
  uncharted. Mirror turns the 3am noise into something you can actually
  understand about yourself."
- **Text:** "you're not too much. you're unmapped."
- **CTA:** "Map yourself → trymirror.world"

### 4. Meet the 5 You's  *(Pillar: The five layers)*
- **Hook:** "There are five versions of you. You've only met one."
- **Visual:** Little One looks in the mirror and *five* small figures step out,
  each labeled: personality, mind, emotion, voice & face, the stars. They line
  up, wave.
- **VO:** "Your personality. How you think. How you feel. What your voice and
  face reveal. And what the sky said the day you were born. Five layers. One
  you. Mirror brings them together for the first time."
- **Text:** "five layers, one you"
- **CTA:** "Meet them → trymirror.world"

### 5. The Question No One Answers Honestly  *(Pillar: Perception gap)*
- **Hook:** "Ask your friends what you're *really* like. Watch them get nervous."
- **Visual:** Little One asks The Others a question; they sweat, look away, give
  a polite non-answer. Then the mirror offers a slot: "anonymous." The Others
  relax and speak freely; kind, true words float up.
- **VO:** "People will only tell you the truth when no one's watching. Mirror
  gives them that — anonymous, kind, honest feedback from the people around you.
  The truth you can't get any other way."
- **Text:** "the honest version"
- **CTA:** "Hear it → trymirror.world"

### 6. Your Stars + Your Science  *(Pillar: Cosmos meets science)*
- **Hook:** "Astrology tells you who you might be. Psychology proves it."
- **Visual:** Little One under a soft night sky; constellations connect into a
  personality chart, then morph into a Big-Five-style bar chart. Both glow
  together.
- **VO:** "You don't have to choose between the stars and the science. Mirror
  reads both — your chart *and* your psychology — and shows you where they
  agree. It's uncanny."
- **Text:** "where the stars meet the science"
- **CTA:** "See yours → trymirror.world"

### 7. The Growth Glow-Up  *(Pillar: Growth)*
- **Hook:** "Self-awareness is the quietest glow-up there is."
- **Visual:** Little One, hunched and foggy, looks in the mirror day after day
  (little date stamps). Slowly it stands taller, the fog clears, a soft glow.
- **VO:** "No new clothes. No new face. Just… understanding yourself a little
  more each day. That's the glow-up nobody posts about. Mirror is where it
  happens."
- **Text:** "the glow-up nobody sees coming"
- **CTA:** "Begin → trymirror.world"

### 8. The Group Mirror  *(Pillar: The five layers / Growth)*
- **Hook:** "What if your friend group could actually *see* each other?"
- **Visual:** A small circle of figures, each holding a tiny mirror. The mirrors
  turn inward to the circle; gentle lines of understanding connect them.
- **VO:** "Your closest people know your inside jokes — but do they know your
  inner world? Mirror Groups let a circle share what they're comfortable with,
  and understand each other on a whole new level."
- **Text:** "see each other, really"
- **CTA:** "Make a circle → trymirror.world"

### 9. What's Your Blind Spot?  *(Pillar: Perception gap / Recognition)*
- **Hook:** "Everyone has one trait they can't see in themselves."
- **Visual:** Little One admires its reflection, but there's a small smudge on
  the mirror right over its chest that it keeps missing. A gentle hand (the app)
  wipes it clear; a soft "oh." moment.
- **VO:** "There's one thing about you that everyone notices and you never will —
  because you're on the inside. Mirror is how you finally see your blind spot.
  Gently. Kindly. For the first time."
- **Text:** "we all have one"
- **CTA:** "Find yours → trymirror.world"

### 10. You're Not Complicated. You're Uncharted.  *(Pillar: Recognition — brand anthem)*
- **Hook:** "You're not complicated. You're just uncharted."
- **Visual:** Little One stands before a huge mirror shaped like an unexplored
  map. As it steps closer, the map fills in with soft glowing regions.
- **VO:** "People call you complicated when they mean they haven't taken the time
  to understand you. You're not a problem to solve. You're a place to explore.
  Mirror is the map."
- **Text:** "a place to explore, not a problem to solve"
- **CTA:** "Explore you → trymirror.world"

## Production workflow (script → post in ~30–45 min)

1. **Script** — pull one from above (or write one to the format). Keep VO under
   ~70 words for a 25s video.
2. **Voice (ElevenLabs)** — generate the narration. Pick ONE signature voice and
   reuse it for brand consistency (warm, calm, unhurried — not a hype voice).
   Add gentle background music from ElevenLabs' library, low in the mix (-18dB).
   Leave a half-beat of silence before the CTA.
3. **Visuals** — animate the simple figures in whatever you're comfortable with
   (Canva, CapCut, or an AI animation tool). Reuse a template: same figures,
   same palette, same mirror prop. Consistency > polish. One idea per video.
4. **Captions** — burn in captions (80%+ watch muted). Big, centered, one line
   at a time.
5. **Export** — 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920.
6. **Post** — same clip to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Write a
   native caption per platform (see below).

**Batch it:** produce a week's 5 videos in one 2–3 hour session, then schedule.
Never produce and post same-day daily — it burns you out and the quality drops.

---

## Posting cadence + platforms

- **5 videos/week**, one per weekday. Reuse each across TikTok + Reels + Shorts.
- **Best pillars per platform:** TikTok loves Recognition + Perception-gap;
  Instagram loves the aesthetic Cosmos/Growth ones; YouTube Shorts rewards the
  "5 You's"/explainer ones.
- **Caption formula:** a one-line hook that repeats the feeling + one question to
  drive comments ("what's your blind spot?") + 3–5 niche hashtags
  (#selfawareness #personalitytype #shadowwork #astrology #mbti — match the
  video's pillar). Put the link in bio, not the caption.
- **Engage for 15 min after posting** — reply to every early comment. The
  algorithm watches early engagement, and this is also where warm leads start
  conversations.

## Daily execution plan (~45–75 min/day)

| Block | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Post & prime** | 15 min | Post today's pre-made video to all 3 platforms with native captions. |
| **Engage** | 15 min | Reply to every comment/DM from the last 24h. Comment thoughtfully on 5 creators in your niche. |
| **Outreach** | 15–30 min | One partner-outreach block from the PARTNER-OUTREACH-KIT (therapists Mon, coaches Tue, creators Wed, retreats Thu, follow-ups Fri). |
| **Watchlist** | 10 min | Check Postmaster Tools once/week; note which video pillar is performing and make more of it. |

**Once a week (2–3 hrs):** batch-produce next week's 5 videos.

## What to watch (and what to ignore)

- **Watch:** saves + shares (recognition signal), comments (curiosity signal),
  profile visits → link clicks (intent), and waitlist signups.
- **Ignore early:** raw view counts and follower count. A 500-view video that
  drives 10 waitlist signups beats a 50k-view video that drives none.
- **The loop:** whichever pillar earns the most saves/shares, make three more
  like it next week. Let the audience tell you what your "true mirror" message
  is.
