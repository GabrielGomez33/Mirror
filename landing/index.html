Common questions

Is Mirror a mental health app?

No. Mirror is a self-knowledge and reflection tool. It's a complement to therapy, coaching, and personal work — not a replacement for any of them. We make no clinical claims.

What happens to my photo and voice recording?

Facial expression is analyzed entirely on your device — the image itself never leaves your phone. Voice recordings are used only for your personal analysis and are stored under your account, which you can delete at any time.

Who reviews me in TruthStream?

Anonymous Mirror users. You share only the parts of your profile you choose. Reviewers cannot see your name, photo, or contact details. You can't see theirs either.

Do I have to install anything?

No. Mirror is a modern web app that installs to your home screen with one tap — iOS, Android, or desktop. Small download, no app store review, updates instantly.

Can I cancel Premium?

Any time, from your settings. You keep Premium features until the end of your billing period.

What is Dina?

Dina is our own AI orchestration layer, purpose-built for Mirror. It's not a wrapper on a general-purpose chatbot — it reads your personal intelligence and gives grounded, evidence-based coaching. In groups, it can help members compare, decide, and understand each other.
